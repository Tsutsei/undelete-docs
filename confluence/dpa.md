This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of use]({{ '/confluence/terms/' | relative_url }}) between the customer installing **Undelete – Trash Audit & Restore for Confluence** ("Customer", controller) and **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, IČO 21540951, 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("Vendor", processor), and applies to personal data processed by the App under Article 28 of the GDPR.

1. **Subject matter and duration.** Keeping an index of the page tree, an activity log and restorable copies of the Customer's deleted Confluence content and restoring it on the Customer's request, for as long as the App is installed, plus the retention period chosen by the Customer.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Automated indexing of page locations, logging of deletions, restores and moves, and copying, storage, display and restoration or re-creation of deleted pages, blog posts, comments and attachments, solely to provide the App's functionality.
3. **Types of personal data.** Atlassian account IDs (e.g. who deleted or restored content, authors referenced in copies) and any personal data the Customer's users put into deleted pages, blog posts, comments and attachments of protected spaces.
4. **Data subjects.** The Customer's Confluence users and any persons mentioned in the Customer's Confluence content.
5. **Instructions.** The Vendor processes personal data only to operate the App as configured by the Customer (protected spaces, retention, attachment backups) and on documented instructions sent to [t.buchta@post.cz](mailto:t.buchta@post.cz).
6. **Where data is processed.** Exclusively within Atlassian's Forge platform (Forge SQL, Key-Value Store and Object Store) for the Customer's site, inheriting Atlassian's data residency. The App sends no data outside Atlassian. The Vendor has no access to the stored data.
7. **Sub-processor.** Atlassian (provider of the Forge platform), under Atlassian's own terms and data processing addendum. The Vendor will announce any new sub-processor on this page in advance.
8. **Security.** Data is protected by the security measures of the Atlassian Forge platform (encryption at rest and in transit, tenant isolation). The App re-checks the permissions of the calling user on every request and stores user references as account IDs only.
9. **Confidentiality.** Only the Vendor works on the App and is bound by confidentiality.
10. **Assistance.** The Vendor assists the Customer with data subject requests and data protection obligations as far as the App's design allows (e.g. the Customer can exclude spaces, shorten retention or uninstall the App). Account IDs of closed Atlassian accounts are erased automatically after the weekly personal data report to Atlassian.
11. **Personal data breaches.** The Vendor notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting the App.
12. **Deletion.** Deleted content is purged after the retention period. On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to the Forge data retention policy.
13. **Audits.** The Vendor makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA on request.
14. **Governing law.** As in the Terms of use.
